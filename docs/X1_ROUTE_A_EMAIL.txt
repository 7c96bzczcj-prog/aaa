Dear Prof. Satpathy and Dr. Lareau,

I am Richard Tang, a postdoctoral researcher at Hainan Medical University /
Hainan Academy of Medical Sciences, China. I am re-analysing GSE302113 (Liu et
al., Cancer Cell 2026) and would be very grateful for two items if they are easy
to share.

1) The per-donor union heteroplasmy matrices, or equivalently the Mitotrek clone
   assignments (cell barcode -> clone ID) used in the paper.

2) The numeric source data behind Figures 2E, 2F and S6C (the clone-sharing
   heatmaps).

On (1): GSE302113 deposits one heteroplasmy matrix per library, each restricted
to that library's own mgatk-selected variants. The Methods describe building "a
cell by variant heteroplasmy matrix combining all samples for each donor", and it
is that union matrix a cross-compartment analysis needs. A variant selected in
tumour and NILT but not in blood has no column in the blood library, so absence
from blood cannot be distinguished from absence of ascertainment. Restricting to
variants selected in all three compartments does not solve it, because that set is
blood-present by construction (for SU-L-005, 0 of 507 such variants have zero
blood carriers).

On (2): the heatmap panels include NK/ILC as an annotated cell type, but the NK
entries are not quantified in the text, and the deposited supplementary file is
the patient table. We would rather use your values than re-derive numbers that
already exist.

What we are asking: whether intratumoural NK are clonally closer to NK of the
matched non-involved lung than to circulating NK — the NK counterpart of the
myeloid result you report for monocytes, macrophages and DC3, which is not broken
out for NK.

We are happy to take whatever form is most convenient, including just a barcode ->
clone table. I appreciate that mitotrek is already public; the request is only for
the intermediate the pipeline produces.

Thank you for making the dataset available, and for considering this.
