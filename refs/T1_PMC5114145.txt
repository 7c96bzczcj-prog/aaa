27156386 2017 09 15 2021 12 04 1097-4180 44 5 2016 May 17 Immunity Immunity Transforming Growth Factor-&#x3b2; Signaling Guides the Differentiation of Innate Lymphoid Cells in Salivary Glands. 1127 1139 1127-39 10.1016/j.immuni.2016.03.007 S1074-7613(16)30096-6 The signals guiding differentiation of innate lymphoid cells (ILCs) within tissues are not well understood. Salivary gland (SG) ILCs as well as liver and intestinal intraepithelial ILC1 have markers that denote tissue residency and transforming growth factor-&#x3b2; (TGF-&#x3b2;) imprinting. We deleted Tgfbr2 in cells expressing the ILC and NK marker NKp46 and found that SG ILCs were reduced in number. They lost distinct tissue markers, such as CD49a, and the effector molecules TRAIL and CD73. Expression of the transcription factor Eomes, which promotes NK cell differentiation, was elevated. Conversely, Eomes deletion in NKp46(+) cells enhanced TGF-&#x3b2;-imprinting of&#xa0;SG ILCs. Thus, TGF-&#x3b2; induces SG ILC differentiation by suppressing Eomes. TGF-&#x3b2; acted through a&#xa0;JNK-dependent, Smad4-independent pathway. Transcriptome analysis demonstrated that SG ILCs had characteristic of both NK cells and ILC1. Finally, TGF-&#x3b2; imprinting of SG ILCs was synchronized with SG development, highlighting the impact of tissue microenvironment on ILC development. Copyright &#xa9; 2016 Elsevier Inc.
