25500367 2015 04 16 2026 01 28 1097-4180 41 6 2014 Dec 18 Immunity Immunity Human ROR&#x3b3;t(+)CD34(+) cells are lineage-specified progenitors of group 3 ROR&#x3b3;t(+) innate lymphoid cells. 988 1000 988-1000 10.1016/j.immuni.2014.11.010 S1074-7613(14)00437-3 Group 3 innate lymphoid cells (ILC3s) are defined by the expression of the transcription factor ROR&#x3b3;t, which is selectively required for their development. The lineage-specified progenitors of ILC3s and their site of development after birth remain undefined. Here we identified a population of human CD34(+) hematopoietic progenitor cells (HPCs) that express ROR&#x3b3;t and share a distinct transcriptional signature with ILC3s. ROR&#x3b3;t(+)CD34(+) HPCs were located in tonsils and intestinal lamina propria (LP) and selectively differentiated toward ILC3s. In contrast, ROR&#x3b3;t(-)CD34(+) HPCs could differentiate to become either ILC3s or natural killer (NK) cells, with differentiation toward ILC3 lineage determined by stem cell factor (SCF) and aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) signaling. Thus, we demonstrate that in humans ROR&#x3b3;t(+)CD34(+) cells are lineage-specified progenitors of IL-22(+) ILC3s and propose that tonsils and intestinal LP, which are enriched both in committed precursors and mature ILC3s, might represent preferential sites of ILC3 lineage differentiation.
